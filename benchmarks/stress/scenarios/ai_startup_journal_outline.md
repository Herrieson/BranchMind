# AI Startup Journal (Stress A + C)

## Scenario Goals
- Simulate the end-to-end journey of a founder and AI assistant building an AI product from scratch.
- Emphasize BranchMind's performance in deep follow-up questioning and long-range referencing.

## Phase Breakdown
1. **Market Research & Positioning (Rounds 1-10)**
   - Identify target users, pain points, and competitors.
   - Capture core assumptions and quantitative metrics (budget, timeline, success criteria).
2. **Technical Choices & Architecture (Rounds 11-30)**
   - Discuss model selection, data acquisition, and MLOps workflows in depth.
   - Gradually drill down to module-level details and performance metrics.
3. **Development Iterations (Rounds 31-60)**
   - Daily stand-ups covering requirement changes, bug tickets, and release cadence.
   - Introduce unexpected issues (data drift, customer blockers) that require revisiting earlier decisions.
4. **Go-to-Market & Feedback (Rounds 61-80)**
   - Design marketing campaigns and analyze feedback from early users.
   - Track KPI changes and propose strategy adjustments.
5. **Project Retrospective (Rounds 81-100)**
   - Ask the AI to review and synthesize information from every phase.
   - Produce a retrospective report, lessons learned, and a next-step plan.

## Key Information Points
- Initial budget, team size, and timeline.
- Critical assumptions (target industry, pain points).
- Architecture decisions (models/inference stack, data governance strategy).
- Outcomes and evaluation metrics for each iteration.
- Marketing activities and feedback data.

## Failure Mode Reminders
- Forgetting early budgets or assumptions.
- Mixing up architectural details.
- Being unable to cite specific past events or metrics during the retrospective.
